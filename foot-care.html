In-Home Foot Care
for Seniors

Professional nursing foot care brought directly to your home in Greater Vancouver — gentle, safe, and preventative.

Book Your Foot Care Visit

Healthy Feet = Better Mobility & Independence

Our certified LPNs and foot care specialists provide complete nursing foot care in the comfort of your home. No stressful trips to a clinic — we bring everything needed to keep your feet healthy, pain-free, and infection-free.

Thickened & Ingrown Toenail Trimming

Safe, precise clipping and filing — especially important for diabetics and those with poor vision.

Corn & Callus Reduction

Gentle removal of painful corns and hardened calluses to relieve pressure and discomfort.

Fungal Nail & Skin Treatment

Assessment and treatment of fungal infections, dry cracked heels, and athlete’s foot.

Diabetic & Circulatory Foot Care

Specialized care to prevent ulcers, infections, and complications for those with diabetes or poor circulation.

Moisturizing & Foot Massage

Hydrating treatment and light massage to improve comfort and circulation.

Infection Prevention Education

Tips on daily foot hygiene, proper footwear, and early warning signs.

What to Expect

Your foot care nurse arrives with a portable kit and sterile instruments. You relax in your favorite chair while we gently care for your feet — usually in 30–45 minutes. All tools are single-use or properly sterilized.

Keep Your Feet Happy & Healthy

Regular foot care prevents pain, falls, and serious complications.
